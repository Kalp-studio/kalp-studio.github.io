Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026.

What this covers

Kalp runs a small internal tool, “Shuku and Raahi Uploader”, that publishes and measures videos on our own YouTube channel, Shuku & Raahi. It is used only by the studio, for the studio's own channel. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the studio signs in to it.

What it accesses

When a studio member signs in with Google, the tool asks for two permissions on our channel:

  • Manage the YouTube channel (youtube scope): to upload our videos as private, set their titles, descriptions, tags, playlists and thumbnails, and read those details back to check them.
  • View YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): to read view counts, watch time, retention and traffic sources for our own videos.

What we do with it

The sign-in token and the reports stay on the studio's own computer. We don't sell, share, or transfer this data to anyone, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to train AI models. Nothing about the people who watch our videos is collected by this tool beyond the totals YouTube Analytics reports.

Kalp's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token from the studio's computer also ends it.

Viewers of our videos

If you watch Shuku & Raahi on YouTube, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that. We see only what YouTube shows every channel owner, such as total views and public comments.
